The City of La Habra is exploring a ban on kratom and nitrous oxide canisters known as whippets, [according to Voice of OC](https://voiceofoc.org/2026/07/la-habra-eyes-kratom-and-whippet-ban/). The discussion is tied to broader restrictions on smoke shop retail, per the report, and comes as the DEA [moves to temporarily place][dea-temporary-schedule-one-high-7oh-products] concentrated and synthetic 7-hydroxymitragynine (7-OH) products in Schedule I. 7-OH occurs in trace amounts in natural kratom leaf; the products drawing federal action contain the alkaloid at far higher concentrations, through synthesis or concentration.

## What officials described

City officials cited the DEA's temporary scheduling of 7-OH products, along with federal findings of no accepted medical use and addiction risks, as context for the proposal, Voice of OC reported. The report notes that 7-OH becomes opioid-like when synthetically amplified.

Officials also stressed that the lack of clear regulation makes it hard to distinguish natural from synthetic kratom products at retail, which the report says has led some cities to ban all kratom outright. Where La Habra would draw that product line is not yet clear from the report, which describes the city as exploring the ban rather than voting on an ordinance.

## An Orange County pattern

La Habra's move follows Fountain Valley, where the city council voted 4-1 on July 15 to [restrict kratom products above a 7-OH concentration threshold][fountain-valley-restricts-high-7oh-kratom-products] while leaving natural leaf available, with an age limit of 21 and packaging requirements. California officials have separately positioned the state as [taking an enforcement lead against illegal 7-OH products][california-claims-enforcement-lead-7oh-crackdown].

Local governments are increasingly using retail controls aimed at smoke shops, including licensing and product restrictions, to limit kratom sales even where statewide rules are less restrictive, a pattern reflected in the La Habra discussion, per Voice of OC. The trend extends beyond California: two Massachusetts towns [moved on kratom prohibitions][hopkinton-kratom-ban-natick-weighs-prohibition] in recent days, one adopted and one headed to a September hearing.
