The city council in Naperville, Illinois is debating whether to ban the sale of kratom products, the [Chicago Tribune reported on August 19](https://www.chicagotribune.com/2026/08/19/naperville-council-ban-kratom-products-sale/). According to the Tribune, the discussion centers on whether and how to restrict retail sale in the city. The paper described kratom as a plant whose leaves contain the alkaloids mitragynine and 7-hydroxymitragynine (7-OH), consumed as tea, smoked, or taken in capsules, and connected the local debate to the Drug Enforcement Administration's (DEA) move to temporarily schedule 7-OH above specified concentration levels along with synthetic variations.

A different calculation is playing out in Florida. Marion County's Board of County Commissioners delayed adopting a proposed ordinance that would have banned kratom products, choosing instead to wait for federal regulatory action, [352today reported on August 18](https://352today.com/news/257752-marion-county-just-hit-pause-on-a-kratom-ban-heres-why/). County Attorney Matthew Minter cited the DEA's notice of intent to temporarily place concentrated synthetic 7-OH into Schedule I, which he said had been expected around August 5 but had not yet become effective at the time of the meeting. The [order remained unpublished in the Federal Register as of August 18][dea-7oh-temporary-scheduling-order-still-unpublished].

Enforcement practicality drove the pause, according to 352today. "If you're going to focus on the synthetic products, then you're going to have to have a laboratory that's going to be able to test the synthetic products to be able to prove what's in the product," Minter said, per the outlet. Commissioner Kathy Bryant said the issue had advanced faster than intended and that testimony and emails to the board underscored the distinction between synthetic products and natural leaf kratom, a distinction that separates concentrated or synthetic 7-OH preparations from the trace 7-OH naturally present in raw leaf. The outlet reported that the county plans to monitor the DEA's action and explore pilot testing methods to differentiate natural leaf from synthetics, and that separate code changes affecting smoke and vape shops near schools are scheduled for a public hearing on August 26.

The two deliberations extend a string of local government moves on kratom this month. Earlier in August, [Dutchess County, New York moved to ban concentrated and synthetic kratom products][dutchess-county-bans-concentrated-synthetic-kratom-products], and [Oneida County, New York adopted a unanimous countywide sales ban][oneida-county-unanimous-countywide-kratom-sales-ban]. Retail scrutiny of the category extends beyond the United States: a [nationwide Czech inspection sweep][czech-inspection-sweep-kratom-store-violations] found violations at roughly 63 percent of about 1,000 stores selling kratom and similar substances, Expats.cz reported on August 20.
