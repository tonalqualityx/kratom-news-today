The Dutchess County Executive is pushing for a countywide ban on all kratom sales, a step beyond the restrictions on concentrated and synthetic products that [county legislators passed unanimously on August 10, 2026][dutchess-county-bans-concentrated-synthetic-kratom-products], according to an August 30, 2026 [Power & Politics segment from News 12](https://bronx.news12.com/power-politics-the-race-for-new-york-attorney-general-dutchess-county-executive-eyes-full-kratom-ban-hudson-valley-political-headlines). Per the segment, the executive wants the broader ban in place until more information about kratom's effects is available, and the county plans public hearings to better understand the products' local effects.

The measure now on the county's books targets concentrated and synthetic 7-hydroxymitragynine (7-OH) products, a category distinct from the trace 7-OH that occurs naturally in kratom leaf. As the Poughkeepsie Journal and Times Union reported at the time of the vote, that resolution set civil fines of $1,500 for a first violation and up to $3,000 for repeat violations, added a $500 penalty for sales to anyone under 21, and barred online platforms from shipping the covered products into the county. A full ban would newly reach natural leaf products, which the August measure left legal for adult purchase in the county. The News 12 coverage does not specify what form the broader ban would take or when a proposal might come before county legislators.

Dutchess drew a line in August that several of its New York neighbors declined to draw. [Albany County issued a public health order barring kratom sales][albany-county-public-health-order-kratom-ban] that covers natural leaf as well as concentrated products, and Oneida County's [countywide sales ban was signed into law in late August as a joint enforcement sweep seized kratom products at a Utica store][oneida-county-kratom-ban-signed-utica-enforcement-seizures]. At the state level, the legislature [passed a bill in June banning synthetic 7-OH products][new-york-legislature-passes-synthetic-kratom-7oh-ban], sponsored by Assemblymember Phil Steck, which awaits Governor Kathy Hochul's signature. Pressure on that front continues: St. Peter's Health Partners, an Albany-based health system, [issued a release applauding local kratom sales bans and urging the governor to sign similar legislation](https://www.sphp.com/press-releases/st-peters-health-partners-applauds-local-efforts-ban-sale-kratom-products-urges-new). A full ban would move Dutchess from the natural-versus-synthetic line it wrote into county law toward the broader prohibitions Albany and Oneida counties have adopted.

Other states are also acting on kratom. [Massachusetts' emergency order placing all forms of kratom in state Schedule I took effect August 28, 2026][massachusetts-emergency-kratom-ban-takes-effect], and Montana lawmakers advanced a bill to ban kratom, [Yellowstone Public Radio reported August 28, 2026](https://www.ypradio.org/government-politics/2026-08-28/montana-lawmakers-advance-bill-to-ban-psychoactive-plant). The station's coverage places the Montana bill alongside the Drug Enforcement Administration's addition of three 7-OH-related substances to Schedule I; the available reporting does not detail the bill's text, including whether it reaches natural leaf products or tracks the federal focus on synthetic compounds. At the federal level, the [DEA's temporary Schedule I order covers three synthetic kratom-related compounds][dea-finalizes-schedule-one-three-synthetic-kratom-compounds] and does not reach natural leaf products; the full bans in Massachusetts, Albany County, and Oneida County do. The agency's proposed threshold for concentrated 7-OH is still pending, with the [public comment period open through September 10, 2026][hhs-extends-7oh-comment-period-september-10].

The broader ban remains a proposal. The News 12 segment describes it as a push by the county executive, with public hearings planned and no timeline reported, and presents the unanimous ban on synthetic kratom sales as the county's action to date.
