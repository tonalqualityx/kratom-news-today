Dutchess County Executive Sue Serino signed a local law this week that forbids the sale and distribution of concentrated and synthetic 7-hydroxymitragynine (7-OH) products, [News 12 reported](https://hudsonvalley.news12.com/dutchess-county-executive-sue-serino-signs-partial-kratom-ban) on September 12, 2026. The products covered are concentrated or lab-made 7-OH preparations, a category distinct from the trace 7-OH that occurs naturally in kratom leaf. The station described the measure as a partial ban; the broader proposal it reported legislators rejected would have reached the non-synthetic form of the plant as well.

According to News 12, the Dutchess County Legislature voted unanimously in favor of the act last month, and legislators rejected Serino's previous proposal for a full ban that would have included the non-synthetic form of the plant. The station described her signing as a partial ban. The report does not give an effective date for the law.

## What Serino said

Serino signed the measure while saying it fell short of what she wanted, according to News 12. "I do recognize that any step forward is better than maintaining the status quo. To be clear, the adopted legislation does not go far enough, but we cannot delay action. If our actions prevent even one family from experiencing the tragedy of losing a loved one to this dangerous substance, this measure will be worth it," she said in a statement quoted by the station.

News 12 reported that Serino argues all forms of kratom are unsafe, citing the Food and Drug Administration's warnings about the substance. The station's report describes the products as commonly sold at gas stations and convenience stores.

## What the law does

The signed law is the measure [county legislators passed unanimously on August 10, 2026][dutchess-county-bans-concentrated-synthetic-kratom-products]. As the [Times Union](https://www.timesunion.com/health/article/dutchess-county-bans-kratom-sales-22382405.php) reported at the time, the measure sets civil penalties of $1,500 for a first violation and up to $3,000 for later violations, adds a $500 penalty for sales to anyone under 21, and bars online platforms from shipping the covered products into the county. The [Poughkeepsie Journal](https://www.poughkeepsiejournal.com/story/news/local/2026/08/11/high-potency-lab-enhanced-kratom-products-to-be-banned-in-dutchess/91253458007/) reported that the resolution names specific derivatives and extends to future analogues designed to evade regulation.

The September 12 report also answers a question left open at the end of August. A News 12 political segment on August 30 [described Serino as pushing for a countywide ban on all kratom sales][dutchess-county-executive-eyes-full-kratom-ban], with public hearings planned. The September 12 report says the legislature rejected that broader proposal and does not say whether Serino intends to bring it back.

## Where Dutchess sits among New York counties

Dutchess joins a group of Hudson Valley and Capital Region counties that have moved on kratom this summer, and its line falls on the narrower side. [Albany County's ban][albany-county-kratom-sales-ban-takes-effect] and [Rensselaer County's ban, passed 15-2 last week][rensselaer-county-legislature-bans-kratom-sales-15-2], reach natural leaf products as well as concentrated ones. In neighboring Ulster County, a legislative committee [declined on September 3 to schedule a hearing on a concentrated and synthetic kratom ban][ulster-county-committee-delays-synthetic-kratom-ban-vote] that the county executive's office said mirrors the pending state bill. That state bill, [passed by the legislature in June][new-york-legislature-passes-synthetic-kratom-7oh-ban], would ban synthetic 7-OH products statewide and was still awaiting Governor Kathy Hochul's signature as of the Ulster County coverage.

The News 12 report includes no response from kratom trade groups. The line Dutchess drew matches a position the American Kratom Association has taken at the federal level: the group has [supported the Drug Enforcement Administration's temporary scheduling of concentrated 7-OH products][aka-endorses-dea-7oh-scheduling-action] while opposing restrictions on natural leaf, and the Poughkeepsie Journal's August 11 report on the county vote noted that the Global Kratom Coalition had praised New York's 2025 kratom laws as "prudent regulation." Neither organization is quoted on the Dutchess signing in the available reporting.

At the federal level, as KNT reported on September 12, the [Department of Health and Human Services' comment period on the proposed 7-OH scheduling threshold closed September 10][hhs-7oh-threshold-comment-period-closes-30000-comments], and the Federal Register showed no DEA temporary order for concentrated 7-OH as of that date. Elsewhere in local enforcement, [Tupelo, Mississippi police began enforcing a citywide ban on all kratom products on September 12][tupelo-mississippi-police-begin-enforcing-kratom-ban-september-12], WTVA reported, after a delay tied to a store owner's court challenge.
