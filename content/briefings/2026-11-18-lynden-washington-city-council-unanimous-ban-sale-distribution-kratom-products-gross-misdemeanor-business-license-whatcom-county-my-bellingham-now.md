The Lynden, Washington City Council voted unanimously on November 16, 2026 to prohibit the sale and distribution of kratom products in the city, [My Bellingham Now reported](https://mybellinghamnow.com/news/297792-lynden-becomes-third-whatcom-county-city-to-ban-kratom-sales/) on November 17, 2026. The outlet described the ordinance as covering kratom products generally. The report does not say whether the ordinance treats natural leaf kratom differently from concentrated or synthetic 7-hydroxymitragynine (7-OH) products.

## Penalties

Anyone found guilty of illegally selling kratom in Lynden could face a gross misdemeanor, according to My Bellingham Now. The city can also deny, revoke, or decline to renew the business license of a business that violates the ban, the outlet reported. The report does not mention a penalty for possession and does not give an effective date.

The ordinance was written to support any federal or state kratom laws, My Bellingham Now reported.

## Other cities in the region

My Bellingham Now described Lynden as the third city in Whatcom County to ban kratom sales and reported that the vote follows similar bans in Bellingham, Blaine, and Mount Vernon. Kratom News Today (KNT) [reported on September 16, 2026][bellingham-washington-city-council-approves-kratom-sales-ban-7-0-gross-misdemeanor] that the Bellingham City Council voted 7-0 for an emergency ordinance covering leaf-based kratom, extracts, and synthesized material, with violations treated as a gross misdemeanor.

Ferndale may be next to take up the question. Ferndale City Council member Jon Mutchler told My Bellingham Now that the council has been occupied with its budget and expects to begin discussing kratom in January 2027.

## Criticism

The bans have drawn criticism from some community members, who say the rules should distinguish between whole-leaf kratom products and synthetic 7-OH products, according to My Bellingham Now. The outlet did not name the critics or specify the basis for the distinction, and it did not quote Lynden officials on the vote.
