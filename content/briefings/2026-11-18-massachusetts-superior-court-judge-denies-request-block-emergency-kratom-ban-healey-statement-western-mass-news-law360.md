A Massachusetts Superior Court judge has denied a request to block the state's emergency kratom ban, [Western Mass News reported](https://www.westernmassnews.com/2026/11/17/massachusetts-judge-denies-attempt-block-kratom-restrictions/) on November 17, 2026. [WWLP reported](https://www.wwlp.com/news/state-politics/massachusetts-judge-upholds-statewide-kratom-ban-following-legal-challenge/) the same day that the judge upheld the statewide ban following a legal challenge. [The Berkshire Eagle reported](https://www.berkshireeagle.com/news/local/massachusetts-kratom-ban-berkshire-officials-treatment-concerns/article_2af1ff29-f668-4a42-8dcf-8984de7dd23c.html) on August 25, 2026 that the state Department of Public Health temporarily classified all forms of kratom as a Schedule I substance effective August 28, 2026. The Berkshire Eagle's description of the order did not separate natural leaf kratom from concentrated or synthetic 7-hydroxymitragynine (7-OH) products. Kratom News Today (KNT) [covered the order and the first legal challenge][massachusetts-retailers-sue-emergency-kratom-ban] on August 26, 2026.

## What the court decided

[Law360 reported](https://www.law360.com/articles/2551008/judge-won-t-lift-mass-kratom-ban-citing-abuse-risks) on November 17, 2026 that the judge declined to lift the temporary ban and cited abuse risks. According to Law360, the judge weighed those risks against the interests of retailers that had to remove kratom products from store shelves.

Western Mass News did not name the judge, the parties seeking to block the ban, or the court's reasoning, and KNT has not reviewed the ruling.

## The governor's response

Gov. Maura Healey praised the ruling in a statement released on November 17, 2026, according to Western Mass News. "I'm very glad to see the court agree with my decision to ban kratom in Massachusetts," Healey said in the statement, as quoted by the outlet. Western Mass News reported that Healey also said the products were sold without basic safety standards and that Healey cited concerns raised by families, doctors, and law enforcement.

## What else the report included

Western Mass News quoted Dr. Ari Kriegsman of the Behavioral Health Network, who said people who use kratom regularly can become dependent on it and go through withdrawal when they stop.

The Western Mass News report included an opposing view from Melody Woolf, who spoke at a Springfield meeting in February 2026 during discussion of a citywide kratom ordinance. Woolf said at that meeting that placing kratom in the same class as an opioid is a disservice, according to the outlet. Woolf's remark concerned kratom generally. The reports reviewed by KNT do not state the position of kratom trade or advocacy organizations on the ruling or on the ban's treatment of 7-OH products.

## Background

Healey signed an emergency order in August 2026 that placed kratom in Schedule I under Massachusetts law, Western Mass News reported. [Law360 reported](https://www.law360.com/articles/2516867/mass-retailers-sue-to-block-kratom-sales-emergency-ban) on August 24, 2026 that four Massachusetts store owners had sued to block the one-year emergency order and argued that the state's public health commissioner exceeded the commissioner's authority in issuing it. The November 17, 2026 reports from Western Mass News and WWLP do not state whether the ruling came in that lawsuit.
