Tennessee Bureau of Investigation (TBI) crime laboratories have received submissions that tested positive for kratom from 34 counties, WZTV Fox 17 [reported](https://fox17.com/newsletter-daily/kratom-found-in-products-sold-under-different-names-despite-tennessee-ban-tbi-says-davidson-rutherford-montgomery-robertson-sumner-wilson-coffee-putnam-warren-midstate-counties) on November 17, 2026, citing the bureau. The bureau confirmed that investigators are finding prohibited kratom-related substances sold under other names, according to Fox 17. Fox 17 did not name the products or say whether the submissions involved natural leaf kratom or concentrated 7-hydroxymitragynine (7-OH) products.

## What the bureau reported

Fox 17 reported that a Tennessee kratom ban took effect on July 1, 2026; the station's report does not cite the statute, and Kratom News Today (KNT) has not independently confirmed the provision. The bureau's count was not broken down by whether a submission arrived before or after that date, according to the station.

The station listed nine of the 34 counties as being in Middle Tennessee: Davidson, Rutherford, Montgomery, Robertson, Sumner, Wilson, Coffee, Putnam, and Warren. The bureau pointed to a recent warning on its social media accounts about substances sold under other names, according to the station.

A bureau spokesperson, whom Fox 17 did not name, told the station: "We have and continue to assist local law enforcement agencies on their investigations involving Kratom." Asked for statewide totals of investigations, arrests, and seizures, the bureau said drug cases can take months or years and gave none, the station reported.

## Related state action

KNT [reported on November 12, 2026][tennessee-department-of-labor-hearing-november-10-add-kratom-drug-free-workplace-program-public-chapter-950-comments-november-24-wztv] that the Tennessee Department of Labor and Workforce Development held a hearing on adding kratom to the definition of a drug in the state's Drug-Free Workplace Program, citing a [Fox 17 report](https://fox17.com/news/local/tennessee-considers-kratom-workplace-rule-change). Fox 17 did not include comment from kratom trade or advocacy organizations in its November 17, 2026 report.
