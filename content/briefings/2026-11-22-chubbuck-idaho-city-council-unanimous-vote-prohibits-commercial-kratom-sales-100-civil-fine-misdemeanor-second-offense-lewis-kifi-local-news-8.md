The Chubbuck, Idaho City Council voted unanimously on November 18, 2026 to prohibit commercial sales of kratom within city limits, [KIFI Local News 8 reported](https://localnews8.com/news/2026/11/19/the-city-of-chubbuck-bans-commercial-kratom-sales/). According to the station, the ban covers kratom products meant for human consumption, including dietary supplements, drinks, food items, and products containing kratom extracts or alkaloids. KIFI's report does not say whether the ordinance treats concentrated or synthetic 7-hydroxymitragynine (7-OH) products differently from natural leaf kratom.

## What the council adopted

KIFI reported that the ordinance makes it illegal for individuals or retail businesses to sell kratom or kratom-containing products within the city. The measure would ordinarily have needed three readings, and the council waived that requirement, according to the station.

A first offense carries a civil fine of $100, KIFI reported. According to the station, a second or subsequent offense within a 12-month period is a misdemeanor carrying a fine of up to $1,000, up to six months in jail, or both. KIFI reported that the ordinance counts each day a violation occurs as a separate offense. The station did not report an effective date.

## Why the council acted

The council cited concerns about health risks and a lack of regulation, KIFI reported. The station did not report which products those concerns addressed. According to KIFI, council members described the measure as a "temporary solution" until the Idaho Legislature or federal authorities address kratom.

City attorney Ryan Lewis, who drafted the ordinance, pointed to action in neighboring jurisdictions. "If the city of Chubbuck is the only one that does not [pass an ordinance], then the very few retail establishments that sell this product will be located in the city," Lewis said during the meeting, according to KIFI.

## Other Idaho jurisdictions

KIFI reported that Idaho Falls, Kellogg, Shelley, and Blackfoot have also restricted kratom sales and that Bannock County is considering a countywide ban. Chubbuck borders Pocatello, where the council voted the next day.
