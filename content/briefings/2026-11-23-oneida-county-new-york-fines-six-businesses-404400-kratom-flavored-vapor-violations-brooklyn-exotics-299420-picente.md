Oneida County, New York announced on November 20, 2026 that its health department had issued $404,400 in fines against six businesses, according to a [county release](https://oneidacountyny.gov/news/oneida-county-fines-businesses-over-400k-for-illegal-substance-violations/). The release states that the fines stem from earlier compliance checks and involve violations of flavored vapor and kratom product sales, improper product location and accessibility, and missing required signage. The release does not say how much of any fine relates to kratom, and it does not say whether the kratom products were natural leaf kratom or concentrated or synthetic 7-hydroxymitragynine (7-OH) preparations. The [Oneida Dispatch reported](https://www.oneidadispatch.com/2026/11/21/oneida-county-fines-6-businesses-over-400k-for-illegal-sales-including-kratom/) the announcement on November 21, 2026.

## The six fines

The county's release lists the following fines:

- Brooklyn Exotics, Rome: $299,420
- 7 Flag Smoke Shop, Yorkville: $56,930
- 315 Deli Inc., Camden: $32,285
- Smoker's Choice, Yorkville: $6,940
- Eagle Street Express, Utica: $5,500
- James Street Market, Utica: $3,325

The release describes the businesses as "found in violation of county and state regulations" and does not cite the specific provisions. "These fines demonstrate that there are real consequences for violations," County Executive Anthony J. Picente Jr. said in the release.

Kratom News Today (KNT) [reported on September 20, 2026][oneida-county-rome-brooklyn-exotics-inspection-184-kratom-products-seized-400-total-eight-actions] that a county inspection of Brooklyn Exotics seized 184 kratom products and 1,950 flavored vapor products. KNT [reported on August 28, 2026][oneida-county-kratom-ban-signed-utica-enforcement-seizures] that Picente had signed a county measure on kratom sales.

## Recent compliance checks

The county stated that a November 12, 2026 compliance check at Waterville Market in Waterville seized 276 flavored vapor products and three kratom products. According to the release, the health department carried out that check with the Oneida County Sheriff's Office, the Oneida County District Attorney's Office, and the New York State Department of Taxation and Finance.

According to the release, 16 establishments have been inspected since July 2026, and the initiative has confiscated more than 7,300 flavored vapor products and more than 450 kratom products. The county listed 7-OH separately among other substances seized during the initiative, along with synthetic cannabinoids, suspected K2, and cannabis products. The release gives no count for 7-OH products.

Neither the county's release nor the Oneida Dispatch report included comment from the six businesses.
