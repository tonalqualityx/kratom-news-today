The Sequim, Washington City Council was scheduled to consider an ordinance regulating sales of kratom products at its November 23, 2026 meeting, the [Peninsula Daily News reported](https://www.peninsuladailynews.com/2026/11/23/sequim-city-council-to-consider-ordinance-to-regulate-kratom/) that morning. According to the newspaper, Dr. Allison Berry, the health officer for Clallam and Jefferson counties, told council members that the products found in local stores and online are typically synthetic kratom, which she described as sometimes 100 times stronger than the kratom plant. Berry also told the council that the most recent of seven Clallam County deaths in which kratom was a contributing factor involved 7-hydroxymitragynine (7-OH) and alcohol, and the newspaper described the product as 7-OH tablets, a synthetic kratom product. The report does not specify whether the other six deaths involved concentrated or synthetic 7-OH preparations or natural leaf kratom. The report was published before the meeting and does not describe the draft's text.

## How the proposal reached the council

Council members agreed on November 9, 2026 to have City Attorney Kristina Nelson-Gross prepare an ordinance using existing ordinances from other Washington cities as a framework, the newspaper reported. According to the Peninsula Daily News, businesses found selling the products under those cities' regulations could lose their business license. The newspaper reported that concern about a lack of regulation and about children's access to the products prompted the proposal.

## What the health officer told the council

Berry addressed the council on October 28, 2026, the newspaper reported. She said a wide array of products is marketed as kratom with little to no regulation, according to the report. "Because they're unregulated, people don't know what they're getting," Berry said, according to the newspaper.

Berry and Clallam County Coroner Rebecca Shankles said the county has recorded seven deaths in the last seven years in which kratom was a contributing factor, including two so far in 2026, the Peninsula Daily News reported. In the most recent death, Berry said, 7-OH and alcohol were the only substances in the person's system, according to the newspaper.

According to the newspaper, Berry also asked council members to keep the issue in perspective, saying other drugs have a much higher death rate. "Kids in Washington state can't walk to a gas station to get cannabis," she said, according to the report. "Kratom, they can." Berry said age restrictions in local legislation could limit children's access, the newspaper reported.

## Other positions

Allison Smith, director of government affairs for the Global Kratom Coalition, spoke during public comment on October 28, 2026 and encouraged the council to distinguish in legislation between synthetic substances labeled as kratom and botanical kratom, the Peninsula Daily News reported.

Council member Dan Butler said he favors local legislation, according to the newspaper. "It won't make a big difference if they go to Carlsborg, but it makes a big statement to kids that they can't go to a gas station to get it," Butler said, according to the report. Sequim Police Chief Mike Hill told the council that kratom rarely comes up in situations in the city, the newspaper reported.

The report included no comment from Sequim retailers.
