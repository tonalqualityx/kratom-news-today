Buyers of 7Tabz brand products would receive payments of up to $185 under a proposed class action settlement, according to a [court-authorized notice](https://markets.financialcontent.com/wral/article/gnwcq-2026-11-23-class-action-settlement-involving-purchasers-of-7tabz-brand-products-from-january-1-2024-through-october-23-2026) published on November 23, 2026. The notice describes the products as containing 7-hydroxymitragynine (7-OH) or mitragynine pseudoindoxyl. It does not describe them as natural leaf kratom, which contains 7-OH only in trace amounts, and it does not state whether the 7-OH in the products is concentrated from leaf or synthesized. The notice states that the payments depend on the court granting final approval.

RG/2 Claims Administration LLC, the settlement administrator, issued the notice. It states that a state court authorized it.

## The case

The notice identifies the case as S.C. v. 7Tabz Retail, LLC et al., Case No. 26CU001419N, in the Superior Court of California, County of San Diego. The defendants are 7Tabz Retail, LLC and 7Tabz Distribution, LLC. The plaintiff is identified only by the initials S.C.

According to the notice, the suit alleges that the defendants failed to disclose risks of addiction, dependence, and withdrawal associated with the products. The claims are brought under California's Unfair Competition Law, False Advertising Law, and Consumers Legal Remedies Act, along with claims of fraudulent omission and unjust enrichment.

The notice states that the defendants deny wrongdoing and that no court has decided which side is right.

## Who is covered and what the settlement would pay

The notice defines the class as all persons in the United States who bought one or more 7Tabz products for personal use, and not for resale, from January 1, 2024 through October 23, 2026.

Payments described in the notice depend on how a class member bought the products:

- Buyers who purchased directly from the company would receive $100 automatically, with no claim required, and could claim up to $85 more for documented purchases, for a maximum of $185.
- Buyers who purchased only at retail stores could claim $40 on a sworn statement without proof of purchase, and up to $85 more with documentation, for a maximum of $125. The notice states that retail-only buyers who do not file a claim receive no payment.

The notice does not state a total settlement amount.

## Dates and fees

The notice sets January 22, 2027 as the deadline to file a claim, opt out, or object. A final approval hearing is scheduled for March 19, 2027 at the court's Vista courthouse, and the notice states that the date may change.

Class counsel, Lynch Carpenter, LLP, will ask the court for up to $1 million in fees and costs, and the plaintiff will seek a service award of up to $5,000, according to the notice. It states that the defendants would pay those amounts separately and that they would not reduce payments to class members.

The notice is the only source for this report. It includes no statement from the defendants beyond the denial of wrongdoing.
