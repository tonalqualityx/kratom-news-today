The Pocatello Police Department told businesses in Pocatello, Idaho to remove kratom products from their shelves and stop selling them by close of business on November 30, 2026, according to a department advisory [published by the Pocatello Chubbuck Observer](https://pocatellochubbuckobserver.com/2026/11/25/pocatello-pd-city-of-pocatello-kratom-sales-prohibition-and-business-compliance-timeline/) on November 25, 2026. The advisory states that the city's ordinance prohibiting commercial kratom sales took effect on November 25, 2026. The advisory refers to kratom products generally and makes no distinction between concentrated or synthetic 7-hydroxymitragynine (7-OH) products and natural leaf kratom.

## The compliance timeline

The advisory states that the Pocatello City Council approved an ordinance prohibiting the commercial sale of kratom products within city limits. Businesses currently selling kratom products "will be required to remove all kratom products from their shelves and discontinue sales by close of business on November 30, 2026," according to the advisory.

The advisory states that the city recognizes businesses will need time to comply. During December 2026, the department's Licensing and Enforcement Division will focus on education and on warnings to businesses found selling kratom products, according to the advisory.

The advisory states that businesses found in violation will receive an initial warning and that enforcement action will follow if violations continue. The advisory does not list penalty amounts.

## What the advisory says the ordinance covers

The department's advisory describes kratom as a botanical product containing psychoactive compounds, including mitragynine and 7-OH. It lists powders, capsules, extracts, gummies, tablets, and liquids as forms in which the products are commonly sold.

According to the advisory, the ordinance addresses commercial sales within Pocatello and does not prohibit personal possession or use.

## How the ordinance was adopted

Kratom News Today (KNT) [reported on November 21, 2026][pocatello-idaho-city-council-votes-prohibit-commercial-kratom-sales-chapter-5-8-possession-not-penalized-swanson-kifi-local-news-8] that the council voted on November 19, 2026 to prohibit commercial kratom sales, citing [KIFI Local News 8](https://localnews8.com/news/local-news/2026/11/19/pocatello-bans-commercial-sale-of-kratom-throughout-city-limits/). KIFI reported then that the ordinance would take effect after its passage, approval, and required publication, and the station did not report an effective date or penalties.
